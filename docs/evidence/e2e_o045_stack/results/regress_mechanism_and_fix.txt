## 2. Mechanism at F212N (8653,1078) (RA 266.202216, Dec -29.031621)

Frames: nrcb4 exp 2 (pixel 1484.0, 929.1) and nrcb2 exp 4 (851.2, 928.4); the other four exposures are identical in both arms.

nrcb4 exp 2, grouped fit (group 702):

| id | seed (x,y) | seed flux | fitted flux | fitted position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 57872 | 1484.00, 929.10 | 2.77e4 | +3.78e4 | 1484.0, 929.2 |
| 708 | 1484.84, 930.04 | 6.07e4 | +1.07e5 | 1485.0, 930.0 |
| 57001 | 1486.0, 931.1 | 0 | -1.06e5 (flag 5) | 1485.0, 930.2 |

Rows 708 and 57001 are a +1.07e5 / -1.06e5 dipole (net about +1e3) 0.2 px apart, 1.27 px from the star's seed (0.54 FWHM; the seed dedup removes pairs within 1.17 px).
- ctl (no #1366): the 1.0 px dedup keeps the negative row of the pair, the non-positive-flux ban removes it, the pair vanishes, and the star keeps one row: 3.78e4 (nrcb2 exp 4: 4.77e4).
- opt (#1366): the positive row 708 survives and is refit at its own seed (the net flux is below 0.5 of its flux, so `_dedup_net_positions` does not move it); the refit takes light from the star: rows 3.51e4 + 1.72e4 (nrcb2 exp 4: 3.39e4 + 1.89e4).
- Merge: the merged DAO flux falls from 6.12e4 (ctl) to 4.33e4 (opt).  The faint-replacement guard keeps the DAO row when the satstar flux is below 0.8 of the DAO flux: ctl 0.8 x 6.12e4 = 4.90e4 > satstar 4.81e4, so the guard holds with a 1.7 % margin; opt 0.8 x 4.33e4 = 3.46e4 < 4.81e4, so the guard does not hold and the satstar row (6 frames, qfit 2.97, 0.074 arcsec = 2.4 px from the DAO row) replaces the DAO row (merged flux 4.81e4, qfit 0.017 -> satstar).  Residual of the star: ctl +257/-1598, opt +4811/-954 (opt model peak 3970 at (8655,1080), data peak 5171).
- The ctl arm is marginal for this star too (1.7 % margin on the guard) and the satstar row is a poor fit of its own; the merged ctl row is the DAO row only because the guard holds.

## 4. F480M (410,1680)

Merged vetted rows: opt flux 1070, qfit 0.78, nmatch 4; ctl flux 3976, qfit 0.01, nmatch 2.  In nrcalong exp 6 the group fit of seeds 11006 (3936), 47879 (1253, 1.32 px = 0.51 FWHM from the first) and 11853 gives fitted fluxes 9312, 0 and 3752.  With #1366 the first row is recovered by the refit (3943.8, qfit 0.006, a good recovery of a star that ctl loses in this frame) and the second is kept as a rescued row with its seed 1.32 px from the survivor's seed (refit 107.7, qfit 1.11).  The rescue test in `_dedup_nonpositive_cluster_rows` asks only for 1.0 px.  A rescue separation of 0.75 FWHM (1.94 px) removes the 107.7 row and keeps the 3943.8 row (replay `rgfixD`).  The drop of the merged flux to 1070 was not replayed through the merge; exp 3 has an opt-only row (2197.9, forced refit) that was not replayed.

## 5. Proposed fix (owner PR #1366), diff in `regress_fix_1366_rescue.diff`

Not applied to any branch; tree `wt_rgfix` (detached, from `wt_e2e` 3d7b94cd).
1. `_dedup_nonpositive_cluster_rows(..., rescue_min_sep_pix=None)`: a rescued row's seed must be at least `rescue_min_sep_pix` from the survivor's seed (default unchanged, 1.0 px).
2. New `_dedup_nonpositive_duplicate_survivors(...)`: a survivor whose net flux (own flux plus the non-positive rows it absorbed) is not above the fitted flux of a kept row within `sep_pix` of its seed is a duplicate of that row and is dropped, as it is without #1366.  Survivors are judged in order of decreasing strength.
3. `_manual_phot_pass(..., dedup_recovery_min_sep_pix=None)`; `do_photometry_step_manual` passes `0.75 * ctx.fwhm_pix` (1.76 px F212N, 1.94 px F480M).
4. Unit tests: 43 pass in `test_dedup_nonpositive.py` (seven new).

Replay check of the final fix (`rgfix2A`, `rgfix2B`, `rgfix2D`): nrcb4 exp 2 and nrcb2 exp 4 return exactly the ctl rows (37825.3 and 47710.9, one row each); F480M exp 6 keeps the recovered star (3943.8, qfit 0.006) and loses the 107.7 row.  The survivor drop removes 2 survivors in each of the three frames.  Footprint against the all-switches replay: nrcb4 exp 2, 5 of 54981 rows differ (the 1.7e4 row, a 325 row and three rows below 30); nrcb2 exp 4, 9 of 54820 (the 1.9e4 row and rows of 20-480); F480M exp 6, 19 rows lost and 1 gained (median flux 35; two rows of 450-480 with qfit below 0.11 at x = 2-3, y = 677 that `no1366` also lacks).  Against `no1366`, the fixed nrcb4 exp 2 table still has 61 rows with no `no1366` row within 0.6 px (the all-switches replay has 63), and the fixed F480M exp 6 table has 139.

Caveats:
- 0.75 FWHM is a judgement between the 1.27 px (0.54 FWHM) and 1.32 px (0.51 FWHM) cases here and a pair that is resolved; the pipeline's own seed dedup uses 0.5 FWHM.  A fixed 1.5 px is the alternative.
- Not run on a tile: the o130 F212N tile A/B that #1366 was validated on (leftovers >= 500 at the four render levels 94/20/6/4/3 -> 91/18/5/3/2) and the o045 F212N/F480M tile A/Bs need a repeat with the fix before it replaces the current rescue rule.
- The replay stops at the per-frame table.  The merged flux, the faint-replacement guard and the residual image were not re-run with the fix; the single ctl rows at the star imply the ctl merged row, for which the guard held with a 1.7 % margin.
- A merge-level alternative, not tried: compare the satstar flux with the DAO flux summed over the cluster instead of the merged mean; it would protect against any split star, and it changes behaviour for every replaced row.


# Update of PR #1366 (head 38873cf8): code and validation, as written at the time

## Code (`jwst_gc_pipeline/photometry/cataloging.py`, +132/-18 with the tests)
- `_dedup_nonpositive_cluster_rows(..., rescue_min_sep_pix=None)`: rescued seed at least `max(rescue_min_sep_pix, 1 px)` from the survivor's seed.
- new `_dedup_nonpositive_duplicate_survivors(...)`: survivor whose net flux is not above the strength of a kept row within `sep_pix` of its seed is dropped; judged in order of decreasing strength; NaN and empty input return early.
- `_manual_phot_pass(..., dedup_recovery_min_sep_pix=None)`; `do_photometry_step_manual` passes `0.75 * ctx.fwhm_pix` (1.76 px F212N, 1.94 px F480M). None (default) leaves the switch as before.
- Added on top of fork D's diff: a `keep_in_fit` row is never dropped by the duplicate rule (`dup_np & ~prot`), a NaN test.
- Tests: `test_dedup_nonpositive.py` 36 -> 49 (13 new, names in the PR body). Docs: row of `DAOPHOT_DEDUP_NONPOSITIVE` in `SATURATED_PIXEL_HANDLING.md` (rules, o045 mechanism, new replay numbers, tile A/Bs marked as measured with the earlier form).
