# Radial residual profiles (radprof): summary

Measured (F150W: nrcb1/b2/b3, F250M: nrcblong, exp 1-4, arms main2kf and main2; numbers below are main2kf precap, main2 agrees within about 0.03).

- Own models are the PSF grids evaluated at the fitted positions with flux_fit: the satstar grid (SW fovp512, LW nrcb5 fovp1024; reproduces the satstar model image) and the daophot grid (fovp101). The daophot model image is a 21x21 px stamp per star (verified), so it cannot be used beyond +-10 px.
- Masked satstar core (DQ SATURATED / DO_NOT_USE / NaN, <50 % valid pixels): median 0.077" (2.5 px; 5-95 pct 0.046-0.155") in F150W, 0.158" (2.5 px; 0.095-0.315") in F250M.
- Raw Delta(r) (data - bg - model)/model grows with radius and with faintness beyond about 0.3" for both satstars and unsaturated stars (for example F150W 0.8-1.5": +1.7 at 14-16 mag, +15 at 18-19 mag in the neighbours-removed data). This pattern follows an additive per-pixel offset (fitted dbar about +0.1 MJy/sr at 0.5-1.5", F150W) and not a flux-proportional wing. The headline Delta therefore comes from a per-bin fit  sum(data-bg) = (1+S) model + dbar npix  over all isolated stars of one kind (satstars, or unsaturated), with dbar then removed from every magnitude bin. The raw, uncorrected values are in the "neighbours removed" tables and in radprof_B_raw.png; the raw data without neighbour removal is in the sample=iso / sample=strict tables and radprof_A_strict.png.
- F150W satstars, offset-corrected Delta (precap): 0.15-0.3": +0.16 +- 0.005 (14-16), +0.17 (16-17), +0.15 (17-18), +0.145 (18-19); 0.3-0.5": +0.03, +0.04, +0.01, +0.04; 0.5-0.8": +0.08 +- 0.01, +0.10 +- 0.03, +0.03 +- 0.05, +0.11 +- 0.14; 0.8-1.5": consistent with 0 within 0.05-0.9. The per-bin satstar S peaks at +0.24 at 0.14" and +0.18 at 0.22", and is near 0 at 0.34". The final (capped) flux raises these by 0.02-0.12.
- F250M satstars (precap): 0.15-0.3": +0.08 (15-16), +0.10 (16-17); 0.3-0.5": +0.23 (13-15), +0.19 (15-16), +0.39 (16-17); 0.5-0.8": +0.11, +0.00, +0.23; 0.8-1.5": +0.03, -0.26, +0.20 (errors 0.03-0.2).
- Unsaturated stars (brightest 2 mag below the satstar faint edge): Delta is -0.03 to +0.03 for 0.01-0.1" in both bands, and 0.15-0.3": +0.05 (F150W), +0.01 (F250M); 0.3-0.5": -0.03 +- 0.04 (F150W), +0.14 +- 0.03 (F250M). The unsaturated data is noise limited beyond about 0.5".
- No negative core was measured for unsaturated stars (Delta within +-0.03 inside 0.1"). The satstar core inside the masked radius is not measured.
- Encircled energy (strict isolated unsaturated stars, data/flux_fit vs model/flux_fit in exact apertures at 1, 2, 3 FWHM): F150W 0.590/0.779/0.834 vs 0.579/0.764/0.818 (data/model 1.02); F250M 0.599/0.806/0.849 vs 0.597/0.804/0.849 (data/model 1.00-1.003). The model EE fractions relative to the model within R are 0.594/0.781/0.834 (F150W) and 0.621/0.836/0.882 (F250M), identical to the data fractions within 0.003 in F250M and within 0.005 in F150W (flux-normalised, strict sample).
- Magnitude dependence (task 4): satstar Delta at 0.15-0.3" in F150W is flat across 14-19 mag (0.145-0.167); in F250M Delta at 0.3-0.5" rises toward the faint end (0.23, 0.19, 0.39). Neither band shows growth toward bright magnitudes.

Inferred (not directly measured): the PSF grid matches unsaturated stars in the core and first rings (EE within 2 %), so a uniformly too-concentrated PSF model is not supported by the unsaturated stars. The satstar data exceed the satstar model by about 15 % in 0.15-0.3" (F150W) and 10-40 % in 0.3-0.5" (F250M) while the outer wings agree after the offset fit. That excess is specific to satstars, flat in magnitude in F150W, and therefore points to saturation-related effects (non-linearity or charge spill in near-saturated pixels around the masked core) or to the satstar fit being driven by the wings, rather than to a brighter-fatter trend. The earlier 5-23 % annulus excesses at 8-22 px include the additive-offset term that the fit removes here.

Caveats: the offset fit assumes the per-pixel offset is common to the stars of one kind; unmodelled faint sources bias Delta positive at large radius; the F250M faint satstar bin (16-17) has the lowest S/N; the daophot flux_fit used for unsaturated stars is itself derived with a local background annulus of 6-10 px that contains PSF wings.

# Radial residual profiles (radprof): tables

Delta(r) = S in the fit sum_bin(data - bg) = (1+S) sum_bin(model) over stars (weights 1/npix, 4-sigma clipping, error = bootstrap over stars), i.e. a robust flux-weighted version of sum(data-bg-model)/sum(model); the median of the per-star ratios is given in brackets in the aggregated tables. Bins with <90 % valid pixels are dropped for that star. Radii in arcsec of the bin centre. "precap" scales the star model by flux_fit_precap/flux_fit; "final" uses the model image as written. Unsaturated stars (daophot m7 flux, daophot model image, satstar-residual image) have no precap/final distinction.

Method: satstar data = SCI of the crf (== satstar residual + model where finite); invalid = non-finite SCI/residual/model or DQ SATURATED|DO_NOT_USE. Own model = satstar model image (sat) or daophot m7 model image (unsat), used only for stars with no other source >5 % of the target flux inside 1.0" LW / 0.6" SW ("iso"); "strict" additionally requires no source >1 % of the target flux within rmax+2 px (25 px LW, 50 px SW), which keeps the model image free of neighbour models. Background = sigma-clipped median of the satstar residual image at 1.8-2.4". Unsaturated sample = dolphot-matched daophot stars (flags 0/1) between the satstar 95th-percentile faint-edge magnitude and +2 mag.
