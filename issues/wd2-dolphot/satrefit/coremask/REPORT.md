# Core-mask experiment on unsaturated stars (wd2 NIRCam, F150W/F200W/F250M/F300M)

Label key: (M) measured in this experiment, (I) inferred.

## Question
Does a PSF fit with the core masked return the same amplitude as a full fit for unsaturated stars, as a function of (a) mask footprint and (b) peak level relative to saturation?

## Method (M)
- Frames: the 8 round-7 frames per SW band (nrcb1/nrcb3, exposures 1-4) and 4 per LW band (nrcblong, exposures 1-4), `tree_main2/F<band>/pipeline/*_align_o005_crf.fits`. DQ from `correct_dq_first_group_saturation`; SCI set to 0 where VAR_POISSON is NaN, as in `prep_frame`.
- PSF: the same gridded PSF as the satstar path (`C.load_grid`, `S.psf_in_cutout_coords`). Solves use `satrefit_core.solve` unchanged: annulus background (15-30 px, `LocalBackground`, subtracted, fixed) or joint free constant (`bg: free`); weights are ERR plus the annulus scatter in quadrature (`bkg_scatter_fit_error`), over the 81x81 cutout.
- Star list: the dolphot catalogue (`MAG<band>`) projected through the frame WCS (no per-frame crowdsource catalogue was used). Peak pixel = brightest pixel in the 3x3 around the projected position.
- Selection: no SATURATED or DO_NOT_USE pixel within 3 px of the peak (disk); no SATURATED pixel within 25 px of the peak (so neighbours' saturated cores and bleed do not enter the 81x81 box); no dolphot neighbour brighter than 0.05x the star within 8 px (SW) / 5 px (LW); peak S/N > 50, with S/N = (peak - annulus median)/ERR at the peak. Stars with peak/sat < 0.1 are subsampled to 120 per frame; all brighter stars are kept. The position fit was rejected if it moved by more than 1.5 px.
- Neighbours: dolphot neighbours within the box (down to 8 mag fainter) are rendered with the same PSF at a flux of 10^(-0.4 dm) times the star's amplitude and subtracted before the fits (two passes, the second with the full-fit amplitude). Pixels within 2.5 px of neighbours brighter than 0.02x the star are masked.
- Position: Nelder-Mead on the chi2 of a PSF + constant fit in a 9x9 window (unit weights), starting at the projected position; then held fixed.
- Peak level proxy: peak SCI (MJy/sr, background included) divided by L, the 99.999th percentile of finite, unflagged pixels at least 3 px from SATURATED pixels in the same frame (L = 318-344 F150W, 273-287 F200W, 260-266 F250M, 152-155 F300M MJy/sr). The alternative suggested in the task (p99 of pixels adjacent to SATURATED pixels) gives lower values (237-291, 208-241, 106-110, 68-71), because those pixels span a wide range of levels. The ">0.9" bin means peak > 0.9 L; it is a proxy for the fraction of full well, not a measured fraction of the per-pixel saturation level (no ramp information used). Stars with peak/L above 1 do not occur in the sample (unsaturated by construction).
- Masks, centred on the fitted position: circular r < 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10 px; model threshold, unit PSF > f x peak with f = 0.5, 0.2, 0.1, 0.05, 0.02, 0.01, 0.005, 0.002 (the last three were added after a first pass so that the masked area spans the satstar footprints; the first pass with f down to 0.02 and r up to 4 is in `old1/` and agrees with the numbers here where the masks overlap).
- R = a_mask / a_full with the same background mode in numerator and denominator. Bin medians with bootstrap SE (300 resamples).
- dm of the full fit = -2.5 log10(a_full) - MAG(dolphot) - ZP, with ZP the median over stars with 0.05 <= peak/L < 0.3 per (band, detector). This ZP differs from the benchmark ZP (unsaturated stars at p95+1..+3 mag); the table shows the trend with peak level, not an absolute offset.
- Satstar footprints: round-7 out7 tables (`nfit`, `nrim_fit`) and the satstar catalogues (`sat_area`), matched to dolphot with `capbind/map_<band>.pkl`. `nfit` is about 6500-6560 of the 6561 box pixels: the production fit masks only the zeroframe deep core (plus neighbours) and re-includes about 90-280 recovered rim pixels. I therefore use two areas: A_deficit = 6561 - nfit (the pixels actually left out, including any neighbour masks, so an upper bound for the star's own mask) and A_est = pi (sqrt(sat_area/pi) + buf)^2 (the mask if the whole saturated footprint plus buffer were masked with no rim recovery).
- Production `wingcal_ratio` (catalogue column) is 1.000 for essentially all satstars in these bins (F200W 14-16: 1.003; F200W 13-14: 1.04, N=32), so no radius-dependent wing correction of the size measured here is applied in the catalogue amplitudes (M; `wingcal_check.txt`).

## Key results

1. Small masks (r <= 2 px, f >= 0.1; 3-13 px) leave the amplitude of unsaturated stars within about 1 % of the full fit in F150W and F200W at all peak levels (annulus bg: R = 0.989-1.012; F150W r<2: 0.996 at peak/L > 0.9; F200W r<2: 1.006). (M)
2. In the LW bands R depends on peak level. F250M, annulus bg, r<1 px: R = 0.930 (<0.1), 0.942, 0.966, 0.991, 0.998, 1.000 (>0.9). F300M r<1: 0.946 -> 1.006. At r<2 px: F250M 1.020 (<0.1) -> 1.085 (>0.9); F300M 0.951 -> 1.052. The SW bands show no such trend. (M) The rise of R with peak level is the sign expected from a flux-dependent core deficit (brighter-fatter or similar): the core reads progressively lower than the PSF model scaled to the wings. (I)
3. Wider masks give R above 1 that grows with mask area, most strongly in LW: F250M >0.9 bin, annulus bg, R = 1.30 (r<4, 50 px), 1.41 (r<5), 1.73 (r<8), 1.89 (r<10); F300M 1.24 / 1.33 / 1.87 / 2.04; F150W 1.04 / 1.05 / 0.96 / 0.94; F200W 1.035 / 1.03 / 1.03 / 0.95. For faint stars (<0.1) R reaches 3-7 at r<10 in every band. (M) The faint-star values and the free-background comparison below indicate that much of this growth is background-subtraction bias, since a fixed annulus background of error delta contributes delta*sum(p)/sum(p^2) to the amplitude and sum(p^2) falls quickly as the core is masked. (I) The free-background fit does not show the large excess at >0.9 (F250M r<2: 1.065, r<3: 1.035, r<4: 1.07; F300M r<2: 1.003, r<3: 0.972), but is unstable for low peak levels (R < 0.5 at r >= 3 for peak/L < 0.3). (M)
4. Free-background R is below 1 at low peak level and rises toward 1 at high peak level in all bands (F150W r<2: 0.836 (<0.1) -> 0.977 (>0.9); F200W 0.840 -> 0.992). The joint constant absorbs part of the wing flux when the wings are low in S/N. (M, I for the cause)
5. dm of the full fit against dolphot is flat within 0.01 mag in F150W (+0.004 to -0.001 relative to the 0.05-0.3 level) and F200W (0.000 to -0.003; free bg -0.013 at >0.9). F250M reads faint by +0.010 (0.5-0.7), +0.022 (0.7-0.9), +0.015 (>0.9); F300M +0.014 at 0.7-0.9 and -0.005 at >0.9 (N=75). (M) The faint reading in F250M at 0.5-0.9 matches a few-percent core deficit pulling the full fit down. (I)
6. Central-pixel data/model at the full-fit amplitude, peak/L > 0.9: F150W 0.986, F200W 0.979, F250M 1.004, F300M 0.989; mean residual in r<1 relative to the model peak: -0.5 %, -1.3 %, -0.0 %, -0.6 %. The residual profile (`fig_d_resid_profile.png`) shows a core deficit of 1-2 % with a positive ring at 1-2 px in F150W/F200W (+0.1 to +0.3 %), and in LW a negative 1-2 px ring and positive 2-3 px ring (+0.2 %). (M) These are small. A 11-16 % core-pixel deficit as reported for saturated LW stars is 5-10 times larger than anything seen up to peak/L of about 0.95 here. (M/I) Whether the deficit grows steeply between 0.95 L and saturation cannot be tested with unsaturated stars.

Predicted bias = -2.5 log10 R(A) for the highest peak bin (>0.9), interpolated in log masked-pixel count. Negative = wing fit reads bright.

- F200W (no observed numbers supplied): A_deficit predictions -0.035 (13-14), -0.009 (14-15), -0.008 (15-16, extrapolated) for annulus bg. (M)
- LW, A_deficit, annulus bg: F250M -0.338 / -0.166 / -0.089 / -0.045 for 12.3-13 / 13-13.5 / 13.5-14 / 14-15 (circ), F300M -0.236 / -0.061 / -0.069 / -0.006. - Overall (I): a LW core/wing mismatch of the sort measured here (R rising from 0.93-0.95 at low level to 1.0-1.09 at high level for r<1-2 px) goes in the observed direction (wing amplitude reads bright, increasing with footprint) and has a magnitude in the range of the observed 0.03-0.05 mag for the footprint that the fit actually masks. The F150W offsets of 0.03-0.04 mag at 15-17 mag are not explained by it. The ratio is not a clean correction to apply: it depends on the background mode (annulus vs free), mask family and area (differences of 0.05-0.2 mag at the larger areas), and it is measured at peak/L < 1.

## Caveats
- The saturation proxy L is a frame-wide percentile of unflagged pixels, not a per-pixel full-well value. The LW L values include bright extended emission; the alternative proxy is 2.5x lower in F250M/F300M. Peak-level bins are therefore ordinal, not calibrated fractions of full well. (M)
- Peak levels above 1 (saturated stars) are not sampled; the satstar predictions use the >0.9 bin and are an extrapolation. If the core deficit grows non-linearly near and beyond full well, the predictions underestimate it. (I)
- The production fit re-includes 90-280 rim pixels recovered from the first read inside the saturated footprint, which this experiment does not emulate. Those pixels carry their own scaling errors (rim rewrite), so the comparison covers only the effect of excluding the core, not of the rim values. (I)
- A_deficit includes pixels masked for neighbouring saturated stars, so it overestimates the star's own mask; A_est ignores rim recovery and overestimates it in the other direction. (M)
- Masked amplitudes at areas above about 50 px depend on the background mode and on the mask family; R at those areas has SE of 0.01-0.05 and a possible background bias. The interpolation in log area between 9 masks per family is coarse; a `*` in the table marks extrapolation.
- Neighbour subtraction uses dolphot magnitudes (dolphot twin-merged rows and saturated neighbours are excluded by the 25 px saturation cut, which also removes stars near saturated stars and biases the sample toward less crowded regions). The dolphot catalogue was not checked for duplicate rows. Positions come from a core-only (r<4 px) chi2 fit.
- The star selection requires peak S/N > 50 on the stars' own peak pixel; the sample at peak/L < 0.1 is the brightest of the faint stars and is subsampled.
- dm numbers are relative to a per-detector zero point at peak/L 0.05-0.3, not the benchmark ZP.
